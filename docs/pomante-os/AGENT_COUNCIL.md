# Agent Council

> **Purpose:** Lightweight protocol for using multiple AI reviews on consequential work.
> **Update trigger:** Review roles, escalation thresholds, or synthesis rules change.
> **Related files:** [Decisions](DECISIONS.md), [Research Log](RESEARCH_LOG.md), [Active Sprint](ACTIVE_SPRINT.md)
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-22

## When to convene

Use independent specialist reviews when a decision is high-cost, hard to reverse, safety-sensitive, or spans at least three domains such as learning, product, SEO, data, and brand. Routine edits do not require a council.

## Roles

- **Owner:** frames the decision and evidence needed.
- **Domain reviewer(s):** test assumptions from distinct specialties.
- **Skeptic:** searches for failure modes and missing evidence.
- **Synthesizer:** records the outcome, dissent, and next action.

Agents advise; they do not manufacture consensus or invent evidence. A council result becomes canonical only when recorded in [Decisions](DECISIONS.md) or [Open Decisions](OPEN_DECISIONS.md).
