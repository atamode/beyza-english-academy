# AI Visibility

> **Purpose:** Improve legitimate discoverability and citation readiness in answer engines.
> **Update trigger:** AI referral behavior, source coverage, content standards, or measurement changes.
> **Related files:** [Editorial Guide](EDITORIAL_GUIDE.md), [Content Clusters](CONTENT_CLUSTERS.md), [KPI](KPI.md)
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-22

## Approach

- Publish direct, well-scoped answers supported by first-party product facts and cited external evidence where needed.
- Make entity names, brand relationships, authorship, dates, definitions, and page purpose unambiguous.
- Prefer original examples, product evidence, and maintained evergreen resources over generic summaries.
- Keep important content accessible in page text with stable URLs and logical internal links.
- Never manufacture citations, testimonials, statistics, or “expert” authority.

## Measurement

Maintain a fixed set of representative questions and periodically record whether Pomante appears, which URL is cited, answer accuracy, referral sessions where observable, and resulting activation. Treat changes as directional; answer-engine outputs vary. AI traffic does not replace Search Console, analytics, or user research.
