# Vision

> **Purpose:** Durable direction and product principles for Pomante and Poma Academy.
> **Update trigger:** Mission, target value, or long-term principles materially change.
> **Related files:** [Brand Guide](BRAND_GUIDE.md), [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md), [Project State](PROJECT_STATE.md)
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-22

## Vision

Build a trusted children's learning world in which practice feels like play, progress is visible to families, and every character, story, game, and lesson supports a coherent learning journey.

## Principles

- Learning value and child safety come before engagement tricks.
- Free experiences must be genuinely useful; paid value must be clear.
- Parents need understandable evidence of progress, not vanity metrics.
- The Pomante world should connect product experiences without blurring brand roles.
- Growth comes from usefulness, trust, measurement, and iteration—not page volume or unsupported claims.
