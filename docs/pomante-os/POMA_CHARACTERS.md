# Poma Characters

> **Purpose:** Canonical registry for character identity and continuity constraints.
> **Update trigger:** A character is approved or their visual, movement, voice, or story rule changes.
> **Related files:** [Brand Guide](BRAND_GUIDE.md), [Pomante Kingdom](POMANTE_KINGDOM.md), [Decisions](DECISIONS.md)
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-22

## Global continuity rules

- Character behavior must remain consistent across application, video, story, marketing, and generated media.
- New traits are proposals until approved and recorded here.
- Asset-specific appearance references should point to the maintained source asset rather than be re-described inconsistently.

## Baby Poma

- **Movement:** can only crawl.
- **Never show:** standing, walking, or running.
- This constraint applies to still images, animation, video, scene directions, prompts, and copy.

## Character registry

Other named character specifications require an evidence-backed asset inventory before being declared canonical. Record name, role, visual reference, allowed movement, voice/personality, relationships, and prohibited inconsistencies.
