> **Update trigger:** A brand role, name, audience, or expression rule changes.
> **Related files:** [Vision](VISION.md), [Poma Characters](POMA_CHARACTERS.md), [Pomante Kingdom](POMANTE_KINGDOM.md), [Decisions](DECISIONS.md)
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-22

## Architecture

- **Pomante:** umbrella brand.
- **Poma Academy:** education product.
- **Pomante Kingdom:** English name for the story/character world.
- **Pomante Krallığı:** Turkish name for the story/character world.
- **Mediablu:** separate; it is not part of Pomante's brand architecture.

Never use `Poma Kingdom` or `Poma Krallığı`.

## Voice

Warm, imaginative, clear, and respectful of children and parents. Learning claims must be specific and supportable. Avoid pressure, exaggerated outcomes, false urgency, and childish language directed at adults.

## Product expression

Characters and play create invitation; the learning path and visible progress provide substance. Turkish parent/teacher communication may explain the experience, while student-facing language follows the learning design. Access, pricing, and limitations must remain plain.
