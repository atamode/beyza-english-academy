## Ownership boundary

- `/` owns platform evaluation: what Poma Academy is and how it uses games, lessons, stories, and progress signals.
- `/ingilizce-oyunlari/` will own playable-game discovery and the general “free online English games for children” need.
- Football and volleyball pages will own their actual game experiences and link upward to the game hub.
- The Knowledge Center must not publish a generic article targeting the same game-discovery phrase. Its first supporting guide should help a parent choose or use an English word game and link to the game hub only where useful.
- Do not create age, grade, or class variants until distinct demand and materially different content are evidenced.
