## Draft

1. Direct answer and who this is for.
2. Essential context and definitions.
3. Actionable steps or decision framework.
4. Examples, limitations, and common mistakes.
5. Short summary and relevant next step.

## Pre-publish gate

Fact/evidence check; original human value; readable title/description; correct headings; useful internal links; accessible media; eligible schema only; analytics event; author/date/review metadata; no unsupported promise.
