# Editorial Guide

> **Purpose:** Human-first quality and governance standard for Knowledge Center content.
> **Update trigger:** Editorial voice, evidence, review, or publishing standards change.
> **Related files:** [Knowledge Center](KNOWLEDGE_CENTER.md), [Article Template](ARTICLE_TEMPLATE.md), [AI Visibility](AI_VISIBILITY.md)
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-22

## Reader promise

Answer the real question clearly, safely, and usefully before asking the reader to act. Write primarily for Turkish families and educators unless a brief names another audience.

## Standards

- Open with the answer and explain terms in plain language.
- Distinguish evidence, product facts, practitioner judgment, and hypotheses.
- Cite authoritative primary sources for claims that need support; record access/review dates.
- Never promise learning outcomes, rankings, or child-development effects without suitable evidence.
- Use examples to clarify, not to pad length or narrowly repeat a prompt.
- Mention Poma Academy only where it genuinely helps the reader.
- Review sensitive education, child safety, privacy, medical, or legal claims with appropriate expertise.

## Content types

Evergreen guides own durable answers and receive scheduled maintenance. Blog/editorial posts are reserved for dated news, campaigns, releases, or time-bound analysis and must link to the evergreen source where one exists.

Each published piece needs an owner, author/reviewer, source notes, last reviewed date, next review trigger, internal links, and measurement plan.
