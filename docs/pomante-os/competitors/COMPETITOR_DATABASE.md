## Rules

- No current product, price, feature, audience, or performance claim without dated evidence.
- Prefer official product/pricing/help pages, then reliable independent evidence.
- Separate observation, inference, transferable principle, and recommendation.
- “Competitor” may mean direct, adjacent, or benchmark; label the relationship after research.
- Review volatile facts before using them in a decision.
