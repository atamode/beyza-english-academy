# Knowledge Center

> **Purpose:** Define the Knowledge Center as an acquisition and trust product.
> **Update trigger:** Topic taxonomy, content type, governance, or quality standards change.
> **Related files:** [Content Clusters](CONTENT_CLUSTERS.md), [Editorial Guide](EDITORIAL_GUIDE.md), [Article Template](ARTICLE_TEMPLATE.md), [FAQ Plan](FAQ_PLAN.md), [Glossary Plan](GLOSSARY_PLAN.md)
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-22

## Mission

Help families and educators make better English-learning decisions, give children safe and understandable practice paths, and connect that trust to the appropriate Poma Academy experience.

## Structure

- **Topic hubs:** curated entry points for a coherent learning need.
- **Evergreen guides:** durable, comprehensive answers maintained over time.
- **FAQ resources:** concise answers where a real recurring question exists.
- **Glossary:** plain-language definitions that resolve a genuine concept need.
- **Editorial/blog posts:** time-sensitive material with a clear expiry or update plan.

It is not a feed of unrelated keyword articles. Each item needs an audience, intent, evidence basis, owner, review date, internal-link role, and measurable next action.

## Publishing gate

A piece ships only when it is accurate, original in synthesis, readable by its intended audience, useful without conversion, appropriately linked, and assigned a review trigger. Structured data is applied only when page content and search-engine eligibility rules support it.
